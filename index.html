How it works

Three steps to a defensible conclusion.

No black boxes. Each step leaves a record you can review, question, and share.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Bring the question and the evidence. Paste links, drop files, or connect a data source. Veritas preserves the original record before analysis begins.

  2. 02

    Corroborate

    The engine breaks each claim into testable parts, retrieves independent sources, and scores agreement, contradiction, and source bias.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Review the evidence graph, adjust the confidence level, and publish a dossier with a complete chain of custody.
