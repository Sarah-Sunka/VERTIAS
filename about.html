Why we exist

The internet made evidence abundant. It did not make it trustworthy.

Analysts spend more time collecting and reconciling sources than interpreting them. A single report can hide dozens of tabs, dead links, and contradictory claims. When the conclusion matters, “I think so” is not enough.

Veritas was built to make the path from raw evidence to a decision visible, repeatable, and open to challenge. Every claim in a Veritas dossier carries its source, its confidence, and its counter-evidence. Nothing is accepted because it sounds right.

What we optimize for

  • TraceabilityEvery statement links to its original source and the reasoning behind it.
  • Independent corroborationOne confident source is never presented as proof.
  • Time to clarityHours of reconciliation become minutes of review.

Our principles

Three rules we do not trade away.

These principles shape what we build, how we score a claim, and what we refuse to automate.

Evidence over assertion

A confident sentence is not a finding. We show the underlying source and give the reader everything they need to verify it themselves.

Falsifiability by design

Every hypothesis is tested against disconfirming evidence, not just the sources that agree with it. Refutation is a useful result.

Human authority

The system proposes; the analyst decides. Automated output is clearly labeled, reviewable, and never treated as a final verdict.

Security & governance

Security is part of the evidence chain.

A dossier is only as trustworthy as the system that produced it. Veritas is engineered around custody, access, and proof — so a conclusion can survive scrutiny long after it is shared.

  • Immutable audit ledgerEvery query, edit, and export is recorded in sequence.
  • Source integrity checksHashes confirm that a record has not been altered since collection.
  • Encrypted end to endData is protected in transit and at rest, with role-based access.
  • No shared-model trainingCustomer evidence is never used to train models available to other clients.
